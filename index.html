About

Our mission is to provide opportunities for hands-on research and outreach in industrial and applied mathematics in our community.  Open to Cal Poly Pomona students and faculty, we are supported by the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, the College of Science, and a number of sponsors from the robotics industry.

We explore

  • hands-on applications of math in industry
  • robotics
  • kinematics and control
  • computer vision
  • programming
  • machine learning
  • data science
  • modeling
  • simulation
  • visualization
  • creating educational demonstrations, videos, and activities

using

  • linear algebra
  • advanced calculus and differential equations
  • differential and algebraic geometry
  • topology
  • analysis
  • and more
